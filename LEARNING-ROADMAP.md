# Networking Learning Roadmap

1. **Foundations:** OSI/TCP-IP, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, MAC, encapsulation and performance. Exit: explain packet flow from application to wire.
2. **Infrastructure and addressing:** devices, cabling, switching, IPv4/IPv6, CIDR, subnetting, VLSM and IPAM. Exit: design a small LAN and IP plan.
3. **Switching and routing:** VLANs, trunks, STP, LACP, ARP, static routes, OSPF and BGP. Exit: build segmented LANs with resilient paths.
4. **Services and security:** DHCP, DNS, TCP/UDP, ICMP, TLS, firewalls, ACLs, NAT, VPN, IDS/IPS and Zero Trust. Exit: secure and troubleshoot an application path.
5. **Cloud and hybrid:** AWS VPC, Azure VNet, GCP VPC, peering, transit, VPN and dedicated connectivity. Exit: design hybrid connectivity with non-overlapping ranges.
6. **Containers and delivery:** Docker, Kubernetes networking, CNI, Services, Ingress/Gateway API, service mesh, load balancing and CDN. Exit: trace traffic from edge to pod and back.
7. **Operations and architecture:** Wireshark, tcpdump, flow telemetry, automation, IaC, HA, DR and enterprise design. Exit: deliver architecture, validation plan and runbook.

## Suggested weekly cadence
- 2 sessions: concept and diagrams
- 2 sessions: hands-on lab and packet inspection
- 1 session: troubleshooting and interview explanation
- 1 session: architecture documentation and review

Measure progress by demonstrated outcomes, not hours watched.
